## Defaults (proposals)

- Format: us-modern-metric, version 0.1; source: supplied production requirements
- Trim: 170 × 260 mm; bleed: 3 mm each edge; safe inset: 10 mm each edge
- Printer confirmation: OPEN
- Reading direction: left-to-right
- Binding: saddle stitch; page-count multiple: 4
- Default panels per story page: 5
- Preferred panel range: 3–6; deviations allowed with reason

## Authority and open decisions

Physical allocation belongs here; narrative intent remains in Story and visual identity in Design. Actual page count, advertising inventory, printer requirements and acceptance are issue decisions. Defaults are not automatic approval.
