# Generic 24-page planning example

Contract: comic-production-standard-v0.1-draft; revision: 1; state: DRAFT; acceptance: OPEN.
This generic example demonstrates capacity, not an accepted story or a print-ready issue.

Format: us-modern-metric v0.1. Trim 170 × 260 mm, bleed 3 mm each edge, file extent 176 × 266 mm, safe insets 10 mm (150 × 240 mm). Printer confirmation OPEN. Reading direction left-to-right; saddle-stitch planning assumption, page multiple 4.

**24 total = 4 covers + 20 interiors. Interiors = 16 story + 2 full-page ads + 2 editorial.** Three optional cover ads make five ad-bearing sides overall; do not subtract them from the interior budget again. Editorial allocations are example choices, not required by this contract. The 32-page example uses a different editorial/advertising mix and is not a like-for-like layout comparison.

Default 5 panels; preferred range 3–6. S016 overrides to one panel for a closing splash. Total story panels = 15 × 5 + 1 = 76. Script, storyboard, designs, assets and their revisions remain OPEN; S001–S016 are illustrative expected IDs only.

## Review and adaptation

Budget, slot uniqueness, panel arithmetic and ad references: PASS within this example. Actual Story coverage, visual readability, assets, printer requirements and acceptance: OPEN.

I001 is reserved for title/credits, I020 for editorial matter. Interior ads follow S004 and S011. In left-to-right reading they face S005 and S012; they must not disrupt a protected Story spread. S015 and S016 face one another, so the closing page is visible without a separate reveal turn. Reconcile all placements with an actual storyboard.

To change the extent, set total and cover count, derive interiors, reserve non-Story slots, then remap Story IDs and check facing-page parity. Do not shorten a story or delete editorial content automatically.
